A short post to make it clear where I stand as a person, and with my work.

This website has no tracking or analytics.

I make art, learn, write stories and code without the aid of AI tools. In a decent society AI could be a net good, it is very powerful, but as it stands today it is being implemented in ways that are very dumb and dangerous. AI tools concentrate power in the hands of a handful of people, they promote inequality, job loss, gradual disempowerment, social engineering, they cause environmetal harm, and increase the likehood of events that result in mass-harm(cyberattacks, biological warfare, weaponry, etc).

I follow a plant-based diet. The treatment of animals ought to be done with respect and compassion, but as it stands today, it remains crude and barbaric. That humans have an absolute right to authority over all other animals is erroneous. Universal compassion is the only guarantee of morality.

I am against fascism, racism, sexism, homophobia, speciesism, transphobia, nationalism, ethnocentrism, religious fundamentalism, and oppressive and coercive power structures of all kinds.
